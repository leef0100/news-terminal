        How to read this page

        Typical delay is the middle value of the time between a publisher's own timestamp on a story and the moment it appeared here, counting only stories that arrived while this server was running. A publisher that stamps stories late, or releases them to its feed late, shows up here as delay even though the story appeared the moment it was available. Sources are checked on a fixed timer; a source marked failing is retried on every round and its last stories stay in the feed.

        Halftone describes markets. It is not investment advice, and nothing here is an offer or a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

        Sources: publishers' own feeds for headlines (links go to the publisher); Hyperliquid, Binance, Bybit and Coinbase for crypto markets; DexScreener for token matches; the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (EDGAR), the U.S. Treasury and other official releases for companies, filings, yields and calendars. Prices can be delayed and any source can be wrong or late: the Status page shows what each one is doing right now. Liquidation maps are models, not real positions.
